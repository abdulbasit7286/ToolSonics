URL Encoder/Decoder

Encode URLs to make them safe for web use and decode encoded URLs back to original text. Perfect for handling special characters in URLs, query parameters, and more.

URL Encoding Types

  • Encode URL: Encodes complete URLs (preserves :, /, ?, &, =, etc.)
  • Encode Component: Encodes individual URL components (encodes everything)
  • Decode URL: Decodes encoded URLs back to original text
