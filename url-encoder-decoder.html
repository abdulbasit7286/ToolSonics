What is URL Encoding?

URL encoding converts special characters like spaces, &, %, +, ?, = and other symbols into a format that can be transmitted safely over the internet. For example, a space becomes %20 and special characters are converted into percent-encoded values.

Why Use a URL Encoder?

If your URL contains special characters in query strings or form data, it may break or not work correctly. Encoding ensures that your URLs are valid, safe and compatible with browsers and APIs.
