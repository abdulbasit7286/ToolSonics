Privacy Policy

At ToolSonics, your privacy is extremely important to us. We are committed to keeping your data safe and ensuring complete transparency about how our tools work. We do not store, sell, share or misuse your files or personal information in any way.

All tools available on ToolSonics are built to work securely with temporary processing only, so you can use them with confidence.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

ToolSonics does not collect, store or track the following:

  • Uploaded PDF, image or document files
  • Personal details such as name, email or phone number
  • User accounts, passwords or login credentials
  • Permanent IP address tracking or user profiling

2. File Processing & Security

When you upload a file to ToolSonics, it is processed temporarily and securely. All files are handled only for the purpose of completing the requested action (such as merging, converting or editing).

Once the process is finished, files are automatically removed and are not saved, backed up or accessed again.

✔ Temporary file processing only
✔ No permanent server storage
✔ No manual or automated access to your files

3. Your Consent

By using ToolSonics, you agree to this Privacy Policy and understand how files and data are handled securely on our platform.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact us through our Contact Page.
