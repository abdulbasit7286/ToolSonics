Privacy Policy

ToolSonics respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your data. We do not store, sell, share, or reuse your files or personal information. All tools on ToolSonics are designed to work securely with temporary processing only.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

ToolSonics does not collect any of the following:

  • Uploaded PDF, image, or document files
  • Personal identification data
  • User accounts, logins, or passwords
  • Permanent IP address tracking

2. File Processing & Security

All files uploaded to ToolSonics are processed temporarily in secure server memory (RAM) and are automatically deleted immediately after the operation is completed. No file is saved or backed up on our servers.

✔ No manual or automated access to your documents
