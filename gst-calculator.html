GST Calculator Online – Add or Remove GST Easily

ToolSonics GST Calculator helps you calculate GST quickly and accurately. You can add GST to a base amount or remove GST from a final price instantly. This calculator supports CGST + SGST for intra-state transactions and IGST for inter-state transactions.

How to Calculate GST?

To add GST, enter the base amount and select the GST rate. To remove GST, enter the final amount including GST and choose the correct rate. The calculator will automatically show base amount, GST amount and total amount.

GST Rates in India

Common GST slabs in India are 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%. Some goods fall under 0% or special rates like 3%. You can also enter a custom GST rate if needed.

Who Can Use This GST Calculator?

Shopkeepers, freelancers, business owners, accountants, students and invoice creators can use this GST calculator for quick billing and tax calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I add GST to an amount?

Enter the base amount, select GST rate and click Calculate. The tool will show GST amount and total price instantly.

How do I remove GST from a total amount?

Select Remove GST mode, enter the final amount including GST and choose the GST rate. The calculator will show the original base amount.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

CGST and SGST apply to intra-state transactions. IGST applies to inter-state transactions in India.
