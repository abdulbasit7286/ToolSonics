BMI Calculator - Calculate Your Body Mass Index

Our free online BMI calculator helps you determine your Body Mass Index instantly. BMI is a reliable indicator of body fatness for most people and is used to screen for weight categories that may lead to health problems.

What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a person's weight in kilograms divided by the square of height in meters. It is an inexpensive and easy screening method for weight category—underweight, healthy weight, overweight, and obesity.

How to Calculate BMI

The BMI calculation is very simple:

  • Metric System: BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²)
  • Imperial System: BMI = [weight (lbs) ÷ height² (inches)] × 703

BMI Categories

BMI ranges are based on the effect body weight has on disease and death:

  • Underweight: BMI less than 18.5
  • Normal weight: BMI 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25 to 29.9
  • Obesity: BMI 30 or greater

Benefits of Maintaining a Healthy BMI

  • Reduced risk of heart disease and stroke
  • Lower blood pressure and cholesterol levels
  • Decreased risk of type 2 diabetes
  • Reduced risk of certain cancers
  • Improved mental health and energy levels
  • Better sleep quality and mobility

Limitations of BMI

While BMI is a useful screening tool, it has some limitations:

  • May overestimate body fat in athletes and those with muscular build
  • May underestimate body fat in older persons and those who have lost muscle mass
  • Does not account for factors like muscle mass, bone density, and distribution of fat
