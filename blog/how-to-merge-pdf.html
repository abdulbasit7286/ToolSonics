What Does Merge PDF Mean?

Merging PDFs means joining two or more PDF files into a single file. For example, if you have separate PDFs for certificates, resumes, or reports, you can combine them into one document.

Why Do You Need to Merge PDF Files?

– Submitting job applications – Uploading documents for college or school – Sharing multiple invoices or reports – Organizing scanned documents

A single PDF is easier to manage, upload, and share compared to multiple files.
