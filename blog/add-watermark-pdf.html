What Is a PDF Watermark?

A PDF watermark is a visible text or image placed on PDF pages. It may include words like “Confidential”, “Draft”, company logos, or copyright text.

Why Should You Add Watermark to PDF Files?

Adding a watermark is useful in many situations:
