What Does Add Page Numbers to PDF Mean?

Adding page numbers to a PDF means inserting numeric labels (like 1, 2, 3…) on each page. These numbers can appear at the top or bottom of the page and help users navigate the document easily.

Why Should You Add Page Numbers to PDF Files?

Adding page numbers is useful in many situations:

Page numbers make PDFs look professional and structured.
