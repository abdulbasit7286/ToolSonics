About CODE128

  • High-density barcode format
  • Supports alphanumeric characters
  • Variable length encoding
  • Widely used in logistics and retail

Supported Barcode Formats

CODE128
High-density linear barcode format supporting alphanumeric characters.
Characters: ASCII 0-127, FNC1-4
Usage: Logistics, inventory, shipping
EAN-13
International standard for product identification in retail.
Length: 13 digits (12 data + 1 check)
Usage: Retail products worldwide
UPC-A
Universal Product Code used primarily in the United States.
Length: 12 digits (11 data + 1 check)
Usage: North American retail
CODE39
Simple alphanumeric barcode format with wide industry adoption.
Characters: 0-9, A-Z, space, - . $ / + %
Usage: Industrial, government
ITF-14
Barcode for carton-level identification of consumer trade items.
Length: 14 digits
Usage: Shipping cartons, logistics
Pharmacode
Binary barcode system designed for pharmaceutical packaging.
Range: 3 to 131070
Usage: Pharmaceutical industry
