Reorder PDF Pages

Arrange PDF pages in any custom sequence such as 3,1,2,5

ToolSonics Reorder PDF is a powerful yet simple tool designed to help users rearrange the pages of any PDF file in a custom sequence. Whether your PDF pages were scanned in the wrong order, or you simply want to reorganize chapters, assignments, invoices or notes, this tool makes it quick and effortless. Just upload your PDF, enter the new page order using comma-separated numbers like 3,1,2,4 or 5,4,3,2,1, and download your perfectly rearranged PDF file instantly. ToolSonics never stores your files permanently—your documents are processed securely and deleted automatically after completion. This makes it safe for office files, school documents, ID PDFs and professional reports. Works smoothly on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac. ToolSonics ensures a global, fast and secure PDF editing experience with a clean and user-friendly interface.
