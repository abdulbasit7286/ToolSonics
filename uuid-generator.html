What is a UUID?

UUID (Universally Unique Identifier) is a 128-bit identifier used in software development to uniquely identify information. UUIDs are commonly used in databases, distributed systems, APIs, session tokens and cloud applications.

What is UUID v4?

UUID version 4 is randomly generated using cryptographically secure random numbers. It does not depend on timestamps or hardware addresses, making it ideal for generating unique identifiers securely.
