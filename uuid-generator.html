About UUID Formats

UUID Version 4 (Random)

Most commonly used UUID version. Generated using random or pseudo-random numbers.

  • 122 random bits
  • Extremely low collision probability
  • No time or MAC address information
  • Perfect for most applications
UUID Version 1 (Time-based)

Generated based on timestamp and MAC address for temporal ordering.

  • 60-bit timestamp
  • 48-bit MAC address
  • 14-bit sequence number
  • Useful for temporal sorting
UUID Structure
xxxxxxxx- xxxx- 4xxx- yxxx- xxxxxxxxxxxx
  • 4 = UUID version
  • y = Variant (8, 9, A, or B)
  • Total: 32 hex characters + 4 hyphens
  • 128-bit total size
