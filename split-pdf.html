Split PDF Online

Free PDF Splitter to extract selected pages from a PDF and save them as a new document.

ToolSonics Split PDF is a simple and powerful online tool that allows you to cut or extract specific pages from any PDF file. Whether you want to send only a few pages of a long document, separate chapters from an ebook, or create a smaller PDF from a large file, this tool makes the process fast and easy. Just upload your PDF, choose a starting page and ending page, and download the new split PDF file in seconds. No signup, no watermark, and no software installation is required. All processing happens securely on the server and your files are not stored permanently, keeping your data safe. It works on both mobile and desktop devices, making it perfect for students, professionals, and anyone who works with PDFs.
