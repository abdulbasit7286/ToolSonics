Income Tax Calculator India – Old vs New Regime Comparison

ToolSonics Income Tax Calculator helps you estimate your income tax liability in India. Compare Old Tax Regime vs New Tax Regime instantly and see how much tax you may need to pay.

Old vs New Tax Regime – What’s the Difference?

The Old Regime allows deductions like Section 80C, 80D, HRA and others. The New Regime offers lower slab rates but removes most deductions.

How Income Tax is Calculated?

Income tax in India is calculated based on slab rates. Your income is divided into slabs and each slab is taxed at a specific percentage.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which tax regime is better – Old or New?

It depends on your deductions. If you claim high deductions (like 80C, HRA), Old Regime may be better. If you prefer lower slab rates without deductions, New Regime may be suitable.

Is this income tax calculator accurate?

This tool provides an estimated tax amount based on slab rates. Actual tax may vary due to cess, rebates and updated government rules.

Does this calculator include health and education cess?

No. This is a simplified estimator and may not include cess or surcharge.

Can I calculate tax for senior citizens?

Yes. You can select the appropriate age group to calculate tax under Old Regime.
