What is a Regular Expression (Regex)?

A regular expression (regex) is a search pattern used to match, validate and extract text. Regex is widely used in programming, form validation, log parsing, data extraction, email validation, password rules, URL filtering and many other applications.

Why Use an Online Regex Tester?

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this Regex Tester free?

Yes, ToolSonics Regex Tester is completely free and requires no registration.

Does this tool store my text?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your text and patterns are never stored or sent to any server.

What do regex flags mean?

The g flag enables global search, i makes the pattern case-insensitive, m enables multiline matching, s allows dot to match newlines, and u enables Unicode mode.

Can I test replace patterns?

Yes. You can use replace expressions like $& for full match and $1, $2 for capture groups. The replace output section shows instant results.
